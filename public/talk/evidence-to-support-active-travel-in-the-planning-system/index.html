Evidence to Support Active Travel in the Planning System

Code Slides Video

Date
Dec 7, 2021 1:00 PM — 2:00 PM

This talk, details of which can be found on the registration web page, my colleague Joey Talbot and discuss our prototype ActDev tool. Developed in collaboration with partners from CycleStreets.net, StreetFocus (and associated organisation PlanIt), and A/B Street, the project pushed the boundaries of what can be done with open data to generate an accessible and actionable evidence base on active travel potential and provision in and around proposed housing developments (and potentially other developments).

Robin Lovelace
Professor of Transport Data Science

My research interests include geocomputation, data science for transport applications, active travel uptake and decarbonising transport systems
